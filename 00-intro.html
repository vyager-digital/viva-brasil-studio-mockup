Viva Brasil Travel has something most operators spend careers trying to build: 30 years of destination knowledge and a founder who grew up in Switzerland and built his career in Brazil.

Building a trip is closer to producing a film than booking travel — there is a script, a cast, locations chosen for what they do to people. The itinerary is built around contrast: raw nature against modern architecture, wildlife alertness against hammock stillness. Clients arrive thinking they know what Brazil is. They leave with something they will spend the rest of their lives describing to other people.

Where leads go quiet

That level of care takes time. During peak months — March through April, July through August — time is the one thing Daniel does not have enough of.

Leads arrive faster than proposals can follow. Every proposal is assembled by hand: destination texts, photos, translations, itinerary.

The ceiling is not demand. It is capacity. Some revenue slips away before Daniel can secure it.
