Viva Brasil Travel  ·  A different kind of trip

He speaks his clients' language, understands their culture, and knows Brazil the way most locals do not. Building a trip is closer to producing a film than booking travel — there is a script, a cast, locations chosen for what they do to the people in them. The itinerary is built around contrast: raw nature against modern architecture, wildlife alertness against hammock stillness. Clients arrive thinking they know what Brazil is. They leave with something they will spend the rest of their lives describing to other people.
