Viva Brasil Travel has something most operators spend careers trying to build: 30 years of destination knowledge and a founder who grew up in Switzerland and built his career in Brazil.

He speaks his clients' language, understands their culture, and knows Brazil the way most locals do not. Building a trip is closer to producing a film than booking travel — there is a script, a cast, locations chosen for what they do to the people in them. The itinerary is built around contrast: raw nature against modern architecture, wildlife alertness against hammock stillness. Clients arrive thinking they know what Brazil is. They leave with something they will spend the rest of their lives describing to other people.

The time between a call and a proposal arriving in the client's inbox. That is where leads go quiet.

During peak months — March through April, July through August — leads arrive faster than proposals can follow. Every proposal is assembled by hand: destination texts, photos, translations, itinerary. The ceiling is not demand. It is capacity. Some revenue slips away before Daniel can secure it.

This is what we are building: a private tool that closes that gap.
