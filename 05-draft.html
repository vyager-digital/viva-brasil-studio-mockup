Roteiro: Proposed Route

Day 1-2 | Cuiabá to Northern Pantanal

Arrival in Cuiabá and private transfer to the Northern Pantanal, approx. 3 hours. The road north is already part of the experience: open savanna, increasing wildlife density, the first caiman visible from the vehicle. Check-in at Pousada Aguapé. Afternoon game drive along the Transpantaneira.2 nights, Pousada Aguapé, Northern Pantanal

Day 3-4 | Pantanal: Jaguar Circuit

Early morning boat departure for the jaguar search on the Cuiabá River. The Northern Pantanal has the highest jaguar sighting density in the world. It is not guaranteed, but the conditions here are as good as they get. Afternoon: caiman walk and hyacinth macaw observation from the lodge deck.Continuing, Pousada Aguapé

Day 5 | Cuiabá to São Luís

Return transfer to Cuiabá airport. Afternoon flight to São Luís, approx. 3 hours with connection. Overnight in São Luís.1 night, Hotel in São Luís

Day 6-8 | Santo Amaro: Lençóis Maranhenses

Transfer to Santo Amaro, approx. 3h30 by private minibus. First afternoon: open-vehicle excursion into the park to the Gaivota and Andorinha lagoons. Day two: the full Betania circuit with dune walls, coloured lagoons, and lunch in a small park village. Day three: the walk to Lagoa das Emendadas, accessible only on foot, returning after dark.3 nights, Pousada Rancho das Dunas, Santo Amaro

Day 9-10 | Barreirinhas

Transfer to Barreirinhas. Afternoon boat tour on the Preguiças river. Day two: Lagoa Bonita circuit and cashew farm visit.2 nights, Porto Preguiças Hotel

Day 11-12 | Atins to Parnaíba Delta

Overland through the park by open vehicle to Atins. The following morning: speedboat departure for the full Delta crossing from Tutoia to Parnaíba, with mangroves, crab fishing stops, river beaches, and the red ibis flight at dusk.1 night, Pousada Vila Camurim, Atins

Day 13-14 | Barra Grande: Departure

Transfer to Barra Grande on the Piauí coast. One rest day on the beach before the return journey to Fortaleza and onward flights.1 night, Pousada BGK, Barra Grande
