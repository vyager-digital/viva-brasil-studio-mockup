Roteiro: Proposed Route

Northern Pantanal — Jaguar Circuit

Day 1–2 | Cuiabá → Northern Pantanal

Arrival in Cuiabá and private transfer north — approximately three hours. The road itself is already part of it: open savanna, increasing caiman density, the first birds visible from the vehicle. Check-in at Pousada Aguapé. Afternoon game drive along the Transpantaneira.2 nights — Pousada Aguapé, Northern Pantanal

Day 3–4 | Pantanal: Jaguar Circuit

Early morning boat departure on the Cuiabá River. The Northern Pantanal has the highest jaguar sighting density in the world — I cannot guarantee it, but the conditions here are as good as they get anywhere. Afternoon: caiman walk and hyacinth macaw observation from the lodge deck.Continuing — Pousada Aguapé

Day 5 | Cuiabá → São Luís (domestic flight)

Return transfer to Cuiabá airport. Afternoon flight to São Luís, approximately three hours with connection. Overnight in the city.1 night — Hotel in São Luís

Lençóis Maranhenses — Lagoons and Dunes

Day 6–8 | Santo Amaro: Lençóis Maranhenses

Private minibus transfer to Santo Amaro, approximately three and a half hours. First afternoon: open-vehicle excursion into the park to the Gaivota and Andorinha lagoons. Day two: the full Betania circuit — dune walls, coloured lagoons, lunch in a small village inside the park. Day three: the walk to Lagoa das Emendadas, accessible only on foot, returning after dark.3 nights — Pousada Rancho das Dunas, Santo Amaro

Day 9–10 | Barreirinhas

Transfer to Barreirinhas. Afternoon boat tour on the Preguiças river — slow, quiet, different in character from everything that comes before it. Day two: Lagoa Bonita circuit and cashew farm visit.2 nights — Porto Preguiças Hotel

Parnaíba Delta — River to Sea

Day 11–12 | Atins → Parnaíba Delta

Overland through the park by open vehicle to Atins. The following morning: speedboat departure for the full Delta crossing — Tutoia to Parnaíba. Mangroves, crab fishing stops, river beaches, red ibis flight at dusk. This is the most cinematic day of the route.1 night — Pousada Vila Camurim, Atins

Day 13–14 | Barra Grande: Departure

Transfer to Barra Grande on the Piauí coast. One rest day on the beach before the return to Fortaleza and onward flights.1 night — Pousada BGK, Barra Grande
