Roteiro: Proposed Route

14 days

The route starts with the intensity of the Northern Pantanal, then opens into the visual calm of Lencois Maranhenses and the Parnaiba Delta. It gives you wildlife first, then space, water, sand and coastline, with each transfer becoming part of the story.

Day 1-2

Cuiaba to Northern Pantanal

Arrival in Cuiaba and private transfer north, approximately three hours. The road itself is already part of it: open savanna, increasing wildlife density, the first caiman visible from the vehicle. Check-in at Pousada Aguape. Afternoon game drive along the Transpantaneira.2 nights, Pousada Aguape, Northern Pantanal

Day 3-4

Pantanal: Jaguar Circuit

Early morning boat departure on the Cuiaba River. The Northern Pantanal has the highest jaguar sighting density in the world. It is not guaranteed, but the conditions here are as good as they get. Afternoon: caiman walk and hyacinth macaw observation from the lodge deck.Continuing, Pousada Aguape

Day 5

Cuiaba to Sao Luis

Return transfer to Cuiaba airport. Afternoon flight to Sao Luis, approximately three hours with connection. Overnight in the city before entering the dunes region.1 night, hotel in Sao Luis

Day 6-8

Santo Amaro: Lencois Maranhenses

Private minibus transfer to Santo Amaro. First afternoon: open-vehicle excursion into the park to the Gaivota and Andorinha lagoons. Day two: the full Betania circuit with dune walls, coloured lagoons and lunch in a small village. Day three: Lagoa das Emendadas, accessible only on foot, returning after dark.3 nights, Pousada Rancho das Dunas, Santo Amaro

Day 9-10

Barreirinhas

Transfer to Barreirinhas. Afternoon boat tour on the Preguicas river, slow and quiet after the more active dune days. Day two: Lagoa Bonita circuit and cashew farm visit.2 nights, Porto Preguicas Hotel

Day 11-12

Atins to Parnaiba Delta

Overland through the park by open vehicle to Atins. The following morning: speedboat departure for the full Delta crossing, from Tutoia to Parnaiba. Mangroves, crab fishing stops, river beaches and the red ibis flight at dusk.1 night, Pousada Vila Camurim, Atins

Day 13-14

Barra Grande and departure

Transfer to Barra Grande on the Piaui coast. One rest day on the beach before the return journey to Fortaleza and onward flights.1 night, Pousada BGK, Barra Grande
